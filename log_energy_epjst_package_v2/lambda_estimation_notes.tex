\section*{Essential Verification Checklist}

Please verify the following before interpreting $\lambda$ estimates:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Energy must be computed as sliding window sum of squared returns.
\item Log must be applied to the energy, not to squared returns.
\item Log-energy must be centered before covariance estimation.
\item Covariance must be computed as function of lag $\Delta t$.
\item $\lambda^2$ must be estimated from slope of $C(\Delta t)$ vs $\log(\Delta t)$.
\end{enumerate}

If implemented correctly, white noise should produce:
\[
\lambda^2 \approx 0
\]

\section{Problem 1: Incorrect Energy Definition}
Incorrect:
\[
\log(r_t^2)
\]
Correct:
\[
E_s(t) = \sum_{k=0}^{s-1} r_{t-k}^2
\]

\section{Problem 2: Missing Scale Structure}
Energy must be evaluated at multiple scales:
\[
s = 2,4,8,16,32,64
\]

\section{Problem 3: Non-Sliding Windows}
Energy must move continuously through the signal:
\[
E_s(t) = \sum_{k=0}^{s-1} r_{t-k}^2
\]

\section{Problem 4: Missing Centering}
Centered log-energy:
\[
\\tilde{L}_s(t) = \log E_s(t) - \\langle \log E_s \\rangle
\]

\section{Problem 5: Incorrect Slope Estimation}
Log-energy covariance:
\[
C(\\Delta t) = \\langle \\tilde{L}(t)\\tilde{L}(t+\\Delta t) \\rangle
\]

Intermittency estimate:
\[
C(\\Delta t) \\sim -\\lambda^2 \\log(\\Delta t)
\]
